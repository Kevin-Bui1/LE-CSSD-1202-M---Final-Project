🌱 Shop Green, Live Clean

Small swaps, big impact—make every purchase a step toward sustainability. Sustainable
Yours understands the struggle that comes with finding eco-friendly products, so we’ve found
a solution. Here are some amazing sustainable products we found just for you.




1.

Earth Rated Treat Dispensing Dog Toy

TREAT YOUR DOG: Our treat dispenser is a
simple, playful, and intuitive toy that provides
a fully interactive and rewarding experience
for dogs. Add dry treats or kibble for your dog
to discover as they play.


2.

Balzac's Sustainable Coffee Roasters – Farmers’ Blend

Hearty, country morning brisk, with a clean acidity...And did we mention SUSTAINABLE?
Great Balzac taste just like in the cafés. Simply open the lid, place your favourite
Balzac’s K-Cup pod in the coffee chamber, select your size and press brew. Enjoy!


3.

Nourish Creme Riche

Restore essential hydration while feeding
your skin the finest ingredients that Ayurveda
has to offer. Our best-selling age-defying crème
is clinically proven to reduce the appearance of
redness, fine lines, and wrinkles, while targeting the
signs of hyperpigmentation.


4.

Five12 Sustainable Activewear

Five12 is a performance-wear brand dedicated to sustainability,
using innovative materials to create its products. Recycled fishing
nets, water bottles, and even windshields get a second life as
UV-protected, moisture-wicking shorts and tops.


5.

Tsuno Sustainble Menstral Products

Tsuno offers a sustainable approach to tampons and personal care products,
using bleach-free organic cotton as a healthier alternative to conventional
options. Plus, every box sold supports girls' education in Sierra Leone and Uganda.


6.

Virtue Brush Sustainable Dental Care

VirtueBrush reduces plastic waste by creating hairbrushes
and toothbrushes from renewable bamboo with plastic-free
packaging. Committed to sustainability, the company also offsets
its impact through a tree-planting program, having planted over
62,000 trees.


7.

Flip & Tumble

Flip & Tumble offers reusable, eco-friendly alternatives
to plastic bags, including durable product bags designed
for repeated use. They’re conveniently designed to fit into
the pockets of the brand’s collapsible tote bags for easy storage.


8.

Frères Branchiaux Candles

Frères Branchiaux, founded by three young brothers with their
mom’s support, is more than just a candle-making business—it’s a brand
with a purpose. From the start, they committed to giving back by supporting
organizations that aid unhoused individuals in their community.


9.

Sustainable Made Eyewear

Dick Moby brings sustainability to everyday eyewear with a collection
of biodegradable sunglasses and styles crafted from recycled plastic.


10.

Concious Crafts - Eco-friendly Crafts for the Kids

Conscious Craft inspires creative play and a love for nature with
a selection of kids’ gardening tools, outdoor exploration kits,
and recycled art supplies.
