Jolene's Journey

some words about jolene Hi I’m Jolene! My sustainable living journey has been all about reducing waste and being mindful of what I consume. It started with cutting down on single-use plastics and quickly expanded into trying a zero-waste lifestyle. I’ve made my own cleaning products, switched to a plant-based diet, and even started composting. It's not always perfect, but the sense of doing my part for the planet has been incredibly fulfilling. Sustainability isn’t just a trend; it’s a way of life that I’m proud to embrace.

Dimitree's Journey
